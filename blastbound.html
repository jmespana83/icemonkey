How to play

  • Player 1 — WASD move · Space drop charge · Shift sprint · E mutation action
  • Player 2 — ↑←↓→ move · Enter drop charge · / sprint · . mutation action
  • Kick charges by walking into them. They slide until something stops them.
  • Charges chain. One blast sets off every charge it touches, so a tidy corridor can become a death funnel.
  • Launch pads fling you onto the raised centre. Steam vents flash before they fire — don't stand there.
  • Barrels get thrown by blasts and hurt whoever they land on, including you.
  • Last fighter standing wins. If nobody dies fast enough, charges start raining from the sky.

Mutations

Green pickups help. Red ones hurt. Gold ones do something unhinged to everybody. Purple is a mystery and could be any of them.
